    What this is

    The four pilot locations compare visible change in apartment, villa and community settings. Chart measurements use approximate sampling areas while cadastral geometry remains unverified.

    Where the data comes from

    • Registry. Official project and land registry CSVs from Data Dubai (3,039 projects, 266,258 land records). Al Furjan projects are found through master_project_en; their registry area is Jabal Ali First. Each project is linked to a land record by property_id, then the project ID and parcel fields are cross-checked.
    • Sentinel-1 radar. Earth Engine, IW mode, descending orbit 166. Monthly median image, then the mean VV and VH inside the site circle at 10 m.
    • Sentinel-2 optical. Earth Engine surface reflectance, scenes under 40% cloud, cloud and shadow pixels removed with the SCL band. BSI, NDVI and brightness per image, then a monthly median, sampled at 20 m.
    • Google Earth. Screenshots of Google Earth's historical imagery at the same camera position. Google Earth only has imagery on certain dates, so the image changes when a newer capture exists, not every month. Each frame keeps its real date and provider.

    What it does not show yet

    • Site areas are 60 m circles around a map point (50 m after a 10 m inset), not legal plot boundaries. The Al Furjan point has no confirmed DLD project; Orchid and Verdes points come from Google Maps.
    • There is no trained phase classifier, completion estimate or handover forecast yet. The charts are measurements, not predictions.
    • A matching project ID links two registry records. It does not tell us where the plot is on the map.

    Next

    Draw real site boundaries, label construction phases by hand from the imagery, add more control sites, then test whether a model can beat the simple "time since start" baseline.
