# Historical imagery

On 19 November, four aligned Esri Wayback views were reviewed for Jebel Ali Village.
The dates at the map centre are 21 September 2022, 22 January 2024, 13 May 2025
and 23 November 2025. The sequence shows the old layout, partial redevelopment,
extensive villa structures and later site works. The dates describe captures.
Published project announcements are dated separately.

The images were assembled from Esri WMTS tiles using the same extent at zoom 17.
Date checks at the edges were incomplete. The current World Imagery basemap
returned the same 23 November 2025 image at that extent. Use the links in the
demo's historical archive to inspect source imagery in the provider's viewer.
